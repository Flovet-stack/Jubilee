# Feature priorities

Source: the "Jobaru feature priorities" doc (2026-11-21). Living copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/DqHJZtFuXZsCfyPDAjtHwS. Numbers match the competitor map.

## Must have

- 1 ATS-friendly resume builder
- 2 Work-history library to spin many resume versions
- 3 Job-description keyword match score
- 4 AI bullet and summary writer
- 5 AI cover letter generator
- 6 Job tracker with stages
- 7 Browser extension to save jobs from any board
- 9 Email templates (follow-up, networking)
- 10 Weekly job-search goals
- 11 Designer templates
- 13 Library of real resume examples by role
- 14 LinkedIn profile import
- 15 Multilingual CVs and translation
- 17 AI proofreading and resume checker
- 18 Resume score split by content, format, optimization
- 22 Kanban board view
- 23 Keyword highlighting on job descriptions
- 24 Interview and task calendar with reminders
- 25 Job-search analytics (response rates, funnel)
- 29 One-click ATS form autofill
- 30 Job board with matches and alerts
- 31 Auto-logging of applications on submit
- 32 AI answers to custom application questions
- 33 Detects employer's ATS with ATS-specific tips
- 34 LinkedIn profile vs target-role scan
- 35 Live scoring inside the editor
- 40 LinkedIn headline and About generator
- 47 A/B testing of CV versions (internal)
- 51 Line-by-line bullet scoring
- 54 Country/region CV standard formats
- 55 Fake and ghost job legitimacy score
- 56 Employer responsiveness rating and auto-close of silent applications
- 58 Pay estimates, offer comparison and negotiation script
- 59 Likely rejection-reason report with skill-gap plan
- 60 Writing in the user's own voice, with a genericness check
- 62 Guided onboarding for first-time job seekers
- Gap: country formats beyond the EU
- Gap: timed follow-up and thank-you emails
- Gap: approval-based auto-apply
- A1 Market-aware CV (country, language and ATS detected automatically)
- A2 Quality-gated auto-apply (high-fit jobs, approved in daily batches)
- A7 Recruiter-safe positioning (no spam, no cheating)
- Free CV builder plus paid job-search pass

## Nice to have

- 8 Networking contact tracker
- 12 Resume-to-personal-website
- 16 Mobile-first web app
- 16 Native mobile app
- 19 Chat-based AI agent that edits the full resume
- 27 Blind bulk auto-apply (conflicts with A7; opt-in only if it ships)
- 36 AI mock interviews tailored to company and role
- 38 Coding interview practice
- 39 Interview feedback report with scores
- 41 LinkedIn post generator
- 43 Insider and alumni finder for referrals
- 44 Visa sponsorship filter
- 45 AI chat copilot for search strategy
- 46 Recruiter email outreach (user-approved)
- 48 Opt-in autopilot on the paid pass
- 49 AI professional headshot generator
- 50 Personality sections
- 52 Speech analytics
- 57 Pacing coach, rejection support and pause billing
- 61 Career-pivot advisor for AI-exposed roles
- A3 Referral-first apply
- A4 Learning loop from tracker outcomes
- A5 Interview prep triggered by the interview stage
- A8 Underserved markets (local boards, WhatsApp reminders)

## Not in scope

- 20 Resignation letter builder
- 21 Dashboard for career centers and agencies (B2B)
- 26 Coach view of a candidate's pipeline
- 28 Automatic mass referral emails
- 37 Live in-interview copilot (cheating risk)
- 42 Human career coach marketplace
- 53 Mock interviews with real human interviewers
- A6 Human coach checkpoints
