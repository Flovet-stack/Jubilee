# MVP

Source: the "Jobaru MVP" doc (2026-11-21), renamed for Jubilee. Living copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/2cv7wdSDKndRNS1X9t7gq1

The MVP is 10 features. It tests one thing: will people pay for a pass that turns one good CV into tailored, approved applications? Build time is about 10–12 weeks for a small team (estimate).

## How it was chosen

1. **Job to be done.** "Help me get more interviews without spending hours on each application." Every feature must serve this job.
2. **Riskiest assumption first.** Will users pay for the pass? Users should reach the paywall moment, their first batch of tailored applications, as fast as possible.
3. **RICE scoring.** All 43 must-haves were ranked by reach, impact, confidence and effort (estimates).
4. **Kano check.** Keep the basics users expect (a builder, a tracker) plus one feature that sets Jubilee apart: auto-apply after the user approves.
5. **Thin slices.** Each feature ships in its simplest form, for 2 launch markets and 2 languages.

## MVP features

1. **Resume builder.** A guided editor that builds a clean, ATS-readable CV: no tables or graphics that break applicant tracking systems. Free, and the hook that drives sign-ups.
2. **LinkedIn import.** The user pastes their LinkedIn profile or uploads its PDF. Work history, education and skills fill in automatically.
3. **Country CV formats (lite).** Each CV follows the target country's norms: length, photo, personal details, section order, date style. 2 launch markets only: France and the US (decision 0005).
4. **French and English CVs.** Write once, then produce a natural professional version in the other language.
5. **Match score and AI tailoring.** For any job, a match score with missing keywords and skills. One click rewrites the summary and bullets for that job, without inventing experience.
6. **Cover letter generator.** A short, specific letter per job, made in the same step as the tailored CV.
7. **Job matches and alerts.** A daily feed of fitting jobs from 1–2 sources per market. Alerts by email.
8. **Approval-based apply (lite).** A daily queue of high-fit jobs with CV and letter ready. The user approves each one; Jubilee sends it, or opens the application pre-filled where it can't send directly. Nothing goes out without approval.
9. **Application tracker.** Every approved application is logged with date, company, role and the exact CV version sent. Shown as a Kanban board, one column per stage; users drag cards between columns.
10. **Timed follow-up emails.** A polite follow-up drafted after about 7 days without a reply; a thank-you note on interview day. The user reviews and sends.

Business model: free CV builder, then a paid job-search pass for the weeks someone is hunting.

## Version 1.1 (still must-haves)

- **More CV tools:** role library for many CV versions, designer templates, real CV examples, proofreading and checker, score broken down by content, format and keywords, line-by-line bullet feedback, scoring as you type.
- **More tracking:** calendar with interview reminders, weekly goals, response-rate stats.
- **Smarter applying:** full form autofill, AI answers to custom questions, employer ATS detection with tips, browser extension to save jobs.
- **LinkedIn:** profile check against target roles; headline and About writer.
- **Trust and insight:** fake or ghost job score, employer reply rating with auto-close, pay estimates and negotiation help, likely rejection reasons with a skill-gap plan, writing in the user's own voice.
- **Onboarding and growth:** guided setup for first-time job seekers, email templates, A/B testing of CV versions, more countries.

Recruiter-safe behaviour (no spam, no cheating tools) applies from day one.

## Success metrics

- **Activation:** 60% of sign-ups finish a CV within 15 minutes.
- **Aha moment:** 40% approve their first batch of applications within 7 days.
- **Conversion:** at least 5% of free users buy a pass.
- **Outcome:** pass users report more interviews within 30 days.
- **Rethink signal:** below 2% conversion after 8 weeks.

All targets are starting assumptions to test.
