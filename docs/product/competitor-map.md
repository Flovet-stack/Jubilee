# Competitor map

Source: the "Jobaru competitor map" doc (2026-11-20). Living copy with tables, the 54-feature list, overlaps and the user-research sources: https://claude.ai/artifact/B3JgVg819j3HPgBL9FZLTH

## Summary

- 18 competitors researched: Teal, Kickresume, Rezi, Huntr, LazyApply, Simplify, Jobscan, Final Round AI, Careerflow, Jobright, LoopCV, JobCopilot/Sonara, AIApply, Enhancv, Resume Worded, Yoodli, interviewing.io, Europass.
- Each wins in one zone only: CV quality, apply automation, tracking, referrals, interview prep, or coaching/B2B.
- Gaps Jubilee can own: country CV formats beyond the EU; follow-ups sent at the right time; approval-based auto-apply.
- Strongest combination: market-aware CV → quality-gated, approved applications → referral and follow-up → interview prep.
- Unmet needs from 2026 surveys: fake jobs (67% suspected one), no reply (55% top frustration), mental health (49%), pay transparency (44% won't apply without a range), rejection reasons, sounding human, AI-proof careers, first-time onboarding.
- Auto-apply model: review-first sells better (LazyApply 2.1/5 Trustpilot vs Simplify 4.9/5 Chrome); default to approval, autopilot as an opt-in later.
